03 / ABOUT

An open project. Not a proxy provider.

CR-Hopper collects, screens and republishes a changing selection of public proxy nodes. The servers remain third-party infrastructure; their speed, safety, exit location and privacy cannot be guaranteed.

04 / FAQ

Before you connect.

Is CR-Hopper free?

Yes. The published subscription URLs are free to use without registration; individual servers are operated by third parties.

Which subscription should I use?

Use TXT for Hiddify/URI-compatible clients; YAML for Mihomo and Clash-compatible clients.

Why do nodes stop working?

Public third-party nodes can disappear without notice. Refresh the subscription when needed.

Are these proxies secure?

There is no security guarantee. Untrusted operators may inspect traffic metadata. Prefer end-to-end encrypted connections and avoid exposing sensitive data.

05 / NOTICE

Use responsibly.

For testing, research and personal network use. Comply with local laws and service terms. CR-Hopper does not operate upstream nodes; availability, security, speed, privacy and egress location are not guaranteed. Provided as is.
