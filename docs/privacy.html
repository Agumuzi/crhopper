CR-HOPPER SYNC / GOOGLE DRIVE

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the maintainer-only Google Drive synchronization associated with CR-Hopper. The public proxy subscription website does not ask visitors to sign in with Google or grant access to their Drive.

Effective: October 24, 2026

1 / Who this integration serves

CR-Hopper publishes public third-party proxy subscriptions. CR-Hopper Sync is a separate, privately operated maintenance integration: the project maintainer uses rclone to archive local scanner project files and operational evidence to the maintainer's own Google Drive. Public subscribers do not need a Google account and are not offered this integration.

2 / Google data accessed

When the maintainer authorizes Google Drive access, the integration can read folder and file metadata and transfer project files, including file contents, within the authorized Google Drive account. The permissions displayed by Google's consent screen determine the technical scope of access and may be broader than the specific project paths used in normal operation. OAuth credentials are used by rclone on the maintainer's device, not published in the repository.

3 / How data is used

Google Drive access is used for project backups, one-way synchronization of scanner files from the maintainer's device, recovery, and technical review of operating evidence. The public subscription website does not read Google Drive data. Google account data is not used for targeted advertising, profiling, or sale.

4 / Storage and retention

Synchronized copies remain in the maintainer's Google Drive until the account owner deletes them. Local files, operational logs, and OAuth tokens may remain on the maintainer's own device until removed or rotated by the owner. Revoking access prevents future authorized requests but does not automatically delete existing Google Drive copies or local backups. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

5 / Sharing and disclosure

The integration does not sell, rent, or provide Google Drive account content or OAuth tokens to advertisers or external customers. Files archived to Google Drive are not published through the public CR-Hopper subscription endpoints by this integration. The public TXT and YAML subscriptions are a separate publication process based on publicly sourced proxy candidates. Data may be disclosed if legally required or intentionally shared by the account owner.

6 / Public website

The public site is a static GitHub Pages website without Google sign-in or an advertising analytics integration. A language choice may be saved locally in your browser. GitHub Pages may process ordinary web request information under GitHub's own privacy terms. The site itself does not request Google Drive files.

7 / Your choices

The Google account owner can review or revoke access in Google Account settings and delete synced files directly in Google Drive. The maintainer can remove local tokens, logs, and backup copies from their own devices. Public subscribers can use published links without Google authorization.

8 / Security and Google API use

The maintainer is responsible for protecting their device, Google account, and locally stored OAuth credentials. No private credentials are intentionally distributed with public subscriptions or source files. Use of information received from Google APIs is restricted to the purposes stated here and is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. No system is guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

9 / Contact and policy changes

The maintainer is Agumuzi. For general questions, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public: do not include account data, OAuth tokens, or other secrets. This policy may be updated if the integration or data handling changes; the effective date above will be revised.
